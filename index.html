How It Works

The 3-Stage Dispatch Process

A complete cycle from load assignment to final settlement.

1
Stage One

Load Assignment

Our experienced dispatchers source premium loads and assign them directly to drivers — matching the right freight to the right route every time.

2
Stage Two

Fulfillment & Delivery

The driver picks up, hauls, and delivers the load while we provide 24/7 support — handling routes, paperwork, and any issues along the way.

3
Stage Three

Payment & Settlement

Payment is received by the driver upon successful delivery, then a portion is forwarded back to RTG Solutions — completing the dispatch cycle.
